Student portal

Your +2 identity card, from your own account

Class 11 and Class 12 Science students have a portal of their own — separate from the Kamala Science Campus portal, with its own accounts. Register once, and when the school office has approved you, sign in to keep your details up to date and print your school identity card, signed by the +2 Coordinator and the Principal.

1

Register

Your name, class, group (Biology or Computer Science), roll number, your guardian’s name and phone number, date of birth, address and a photograph.

2

Wait for approval

The school office checks every registration against the school’s records before the account is opened.

3

Print your card

Sign in, add your photograph and your own signature from My portfolio, and print your card — upright or sideways, on A4 or on a card printer.
