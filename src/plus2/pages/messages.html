Message from the Principal

Dear students, parents and well-wishers,

Shree Kamala Secondary School has served Dhungrebas and the villages around it since 2007 BS. For more than seven decades, families here have trusted this school with their children, and our +2 Science programme carries that trust into the two years that decide so much of what comes next.

In Class 11 and Class 12 we ask a great deal of our students, because science asks a great deal of anyone who studies it: regular attendance, honest work in the laboratory, and the patience to understand a problem rather than memorise its answer. Whether you have chosen the Biology group or the Computer Science group, the habits you build here — curiosity, discipline and care — will stay with you long after your National Examinations Board results are in.

To parents: thank you for choosing us. Your encouragement at home matters as much as anything we do in the classroom, and our doors are always open to you.

I thank our teachers and staff for their dedication, and I welcome every student to a school that is proud to prepare the scientists, doctors, engineers and teachers of Sindhuli.

Kamlesh Chaudhary Principal, Shree Kamala Secondary School

Message from the Coordinator

Dear +2 Science students,

Welcome to two of the most important years of your education. Class 11 and Class 12 Science are demanding, and they are meant to be: this is where physics, chemistry and mathematics stop being subjects to pass and start becoming tools you can think with. Whether you are in the Biology group or the Computer Science group, I want you to leave us not only with good marks, but with the confidence to take on any problem.

My advice is simple. Come to every class. Do the practical work yourself rather than copying the result. Solve problems every day, a few at a time, instead of all at once before an examination. And when you are stuck, ask — your teachers are here for exactly that, and so am I.

Your future is closer than you may think. Many of you will go on to further study in science, and Kamala Science Campus, here in Dhungrebas, offers a B.Sc. without leaving home. Wherever your path leads, the foundation you lay in these two years will carry you.

Keep your details up to date in the student portal, carry your identity card, and make the most of every day here. I look forward to watching you grow.

Bharat Malla Coordinator, +2 Science
