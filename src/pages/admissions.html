Admissions

How to apply for +2 Science or the Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) General program at Kamala Science Campus.

Admission dates. Intake for each program follows the National Examinations Board and Tribhuvan University academic calendars, which shift from year to year. Contact the campus office on +977-47-520203 to confirm the current application window and fees before you travel to campus.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Program Minimum qualification Notes
+2 Science
Grades 11–12, NEB		 SEE / Grade 10 completed, with the grades required for the science stream Admission follows NEB eligibility rules for the science stream, including the required grades in Mathematics and Science.
B.Sc. General
4 years, Tribhuvan University		 Grade 12 / higher secondary / equivalent completed Applicants must meet Tribhuvan University's eligibility criteria for the B.Sc. program. 50 seats are available; selection may involve an entrance test or merit ranking as set by the campus and the university.

Process

Applying, step by step

  1. Contact the campus

    Call or visit the campus office in Dhungrebas to confirm that applications are open for your program, and to check the current fee structure.

  2. Collect and fill the application form

    Obtain the admission form from the campus office, complete it, and note any programme-specific requirements the office tells you about.

  3. Submit your documents

    Hand in the completed form along with the certificates and copies listed alongside, plus the application fee.

  4. Entrance test or merit selection

    Where selection is required — for instance when applications exceed the 50 B.Sc. seats — the campus will tell you the date and format.

  5. Apply for a scholarship, if eligible

    If you fall under one of the scholarship categories, submit the supporting documents at the time of admission rather than afterwards.

  6. Confirm your admission

    Complete the fee payment and registration with the campus, and with Tribhuvan University or the NEB as applicable, to secure your place.

Documents to bring

  • Marksheet & transcript Original + copies
  • Character certificate From last school/college
  • Transfer / migration certificate If applicable
  • Provisional certificate If issued
  • Citizenship / birth certificate Copy
  • Passport-size photographs Recent
  • Scholarship documents If claiming

Bring originals for verification along with photocopies for the file. The campus office will confirm the exact list for your program.

Questions

Common admission questions

What are the fees for each program?
As a community-based campus, fees are set locally and are revised for each academic session, so the campus office is the only reliable source. Call +977-47-520203 for the current fee structure for +2 Science or B.Sc. General.
How many seats are available in B.Sc. General?
The B.Sc. General program has 50 seats in total.
What language are classes taught in?
The medium of instruction for the B.Sc. General program is English.
Can I move to the B.Sc. program after finishing +2 here?
Yes. Completing +2 Science — here or at another institution — meets the Grade 12 requirement for the B.Sc. General program, subject to Tribhuvan University's eligibility criteria.
Is there an entrance examination?
Where the number of applicants exceeds the available seats, selection may be made through an entrance test or merit ranking as decided by the campus under the relevant university or board rules. The campus office will confirm this for the current intake.
Do you accept students from outside Sindhuli?
Yes. Admission is open to any applicant who meets the eligibility criteria for the program, regardless of district.
Are scholarships decided at the time of admission?
Scholarship claims should be made during admission, with the supporting documents for your category. See the scholarships page for the categories covered.
