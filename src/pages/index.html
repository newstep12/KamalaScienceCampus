About the campus

A local science centre built with the community

Kamala Science Campus was established in 2066 BS (2009 AD) as a community-based campus in Dhungrebas, Kamalamai, Sindhuli, in Nepal's Bagmati Province. It grew out of a local effort — supported by Kamalamai Municipality and community stakeholders — to widen access to science education in Sindhuli District.

The campus is affiliated with Tribhuvan University for bachelor-level study and is recognised by the University Grants Commission of Nepal. Teaching is supported by laboratories, a library and computer facilities for regular coursework and practicals.

Alongside teaching, the campus extends scholarship support to students from marginalised and economically weak backgrounds, and maintains basic academic and student-support facilities for regular study.

The program

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) General

A four-year, full-time undergraduate degree run under Tribhuvan University's Institute of Science and Technology, taught in English.

⚖

Four years, one major

A broad science foundation in the early years, narrowing to a single major subject with project or field work in the final year.

▤

50 seats, English medium

The campus offers 50 seats in the program, with English as the medium of instruction throughout.

⚑

TU degree, local campus

Curriculum, examinations and certification come from Tribhuvan University; the teaching happens in Dhungrebas.
