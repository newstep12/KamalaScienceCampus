Student services

This website serves two groups of students, from two institutions, side by side in Dhungrebas. Each group has a portal of its own, with its own accounts. Find yours below.

Class 11 & 12

+2 Science

Students of Shree Kamala Secondary School in Class 11 or Class 12 Science — Biology Group or Computer Science Group.

+2 Login Register

  • Your +2 identity card print it after you sign in
  • Your details, photo and signature My portfolio
  • Messages from the Principal and the Coordinator
  • Your teachers
  • Find the school Dhungrebas, Sindhuli
  • About the +2 Science program

Help: the school office of Shree Kamala Secondary School approves +2 accounts and resets forgotten passwords.

Tribhuvan University

B.Sc. General

Students of Kamala Science Campus on the four-year B.Sc. General program — and the campus’s lecturers.

B.Sc. Login Register

  • Notices TU, examination and campus notices
  • Your campus identity card print it after you sign in
  • Learning materials, results and attendance in the portal
  • Scholarships
  • Admissions
  • Contact the campus office

Help: the office of Kamala Science Campus approves B.Sc. accounts and resets forgotten passwords.

Good to know

Two portals, one website

  • Which login is mine? Studying in Class 11 or 12: +2 Login. Studying a B.Sc.: B.Sc. Login. The two buttons are at the top of every page — gold for +2, white for B.Sc.
  • One account does not open the other. The school and the campus keep separate records, so a +2 account cannot sign in to the B.Sc. portal, or the other way round. Register on your own one.
  • Moving from +2 to a B.Sc. here? Register afresh on the B.Sc. portal when you join the campus; your +2 account stays with the school.
  • Teachers who teach both have an account on each portal, given by each office.
  • नेपालीमा पनि: every page and both portals work in Nepali — press नेपाली at the top.
