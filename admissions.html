Admissions

How to apply for the Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) General program at Kamala Science Campus.

Admission dates. Each intake follows the Tribhuvan University academic calendar, which shifts from year to year. Contact the campus office on +977-47-520203 to confirm the current application window and fees before you travel to campus.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Program Minimum qualification Notes
B.Sc. General
4 years, Tribhuvan University		 Grade 12 / higher secondary / equivalent completed Applicants must meet Tribhuvan University's eligibility criteria for the B.Sc. program. 50 seats are available; selection may involve an entrance test or merit ranking as set by the campus and the university.

Process

Applying, step by step

  1. Contact the campus

    Call or visit the campus office in Dhungrebas to confirm that applications are open for the current intake, and to check the fee structure.

  2. Collect and fill the application form

    Obtain the admission form from the campus office, complete it, and note any additional requirements the office tells you about.

  3. Submit your documents

    Hand in the completed form along with the certificates and copies listed alongside, plus the application fee.

  4. Entrance test or merit selection

    Where selection is required — for instance when applications exceed the 50 available seats — the campus will tell you the date and format.

  5. Apply for a scholarship, if eligible

    If you fall under one of the scholarship categories, submit the supporting documents at the time of admission rather than afterwards.

  6. Confirm your admission

    Complete the fee payment and registration with the campus and with Tribhuvan University to secure your place.

Documents to bring

  • Marksheet & transcript Original + copies
  • Character certificate From last school/college
  • Transfer / migration certificate If applicable
  • Provisional certificate If issued
  • Citizenship / birth certificate Copy
  • Passport-size photographs Recent
  • Scholarship documents If claiming

Bring originals for verification along with photocopies for the file. The campus office will confirm the exact list for the current intake.

Questions

Common admission questions

What are the fees for the program?
As a community-based campus, fees are set locally and are revised for each academic session, so the campus office is the only reliable source. Call +977-47-520203 for the current fee structure for the B.Sc. General program.
How many seats are available?
The B.Sc. General program has 50 seats in total for each intake.
What language are classes taught in?
The medium of instruction for the B.Sc. General program is English.
I studied Grade 12 elsewhere — can I apply?
Yes. Any Grade 12, higher secondary or equivalent qualification that satisfies Tribhuvan University's eligibility criteria for B.Sc. entry is accepted, wherever you completed it.
Is there an entrance examination?
Where the number of applicants exceeds the available seats, selection may be made through an entrance test or merit ranking as decided by the campus under Tribhuvan University rules. The campus office will confirm this for the current intake.
Do you accept students from outside Sindhuli?
Yes. Admission is open to any applicant who meets the eligibility criteria for the program, regardless of district.
Are scholarships decided at the time of admission?
Scholarship claims should be made during admission, with the supporting documents for your category. See the scholarships page for the categories covered.

Still have a question?

Send the campus an enquiry and someone from the office can get back to you.
