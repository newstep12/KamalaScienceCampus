Scholarships

The campus extends scholarship support to students from marginalised communities and to students whose economic circumstances would otherwise put a science degree out of reach.

Who is supported

Scholarship categories

Scholarship provision at Kamala Science Campus covers the following groups of students.

⚑

Dalit students

Support for students from Dalit communities, in line with national provisions for inclusive access to higher education.

♀

Girls and women

Support aimed at raising and sustaining the participation of girls and women in science study.

⚔

Indigenous communities

Support for students from Indigenous (Janajati) communities of Sindhuli and beyond.

♿

Persons with disabilities

Support for students with disabilities pursuing a bachelor-level science degree.

☆

Martyrs' families

Support for students from the families of martyrs, under the relevant provisions.

♡

Weak economic condition

Support for students whose household economic condition would otherwise prevent them from continuing their studies.

How to claim

Applying for scholarship support

  1. Identify your category

    Work out which of the categories above applies to you, and what documentation exists to support it.

  2. Ask the campus office what is required

    Requirements differ by category and by academic session. Confirm the current list and any quota before you apply.

  3. Apply during admission

    Submit your scholarship claim together with your admission form, not after admission is complete.

  4. Attach supporting documents

    Include the relevant certificate or recommendation for your category — for example a disability identity card, a ward-office recommendation, or documentation of household income.

  5. Selection and notification

    The campus reviews claims against the available scholarship provision for that session and informs applicants of the outcome.

Please note. The number of scholarships, the amount of support and the exact documents required are set for each academic session and depend on the provisions available to the campus. Nothing on this page guarantees an award — contact the campus office for the current position.
