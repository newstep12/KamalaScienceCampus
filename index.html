About the campus

A local science centre built with the community

Kamala Science Campus was established in 2066 BS (2009 AD) as a community-based campus in Dhungrebas, Kamalamai, Sindhuli, in Nepal's Bagmati Province. It grew out of a local effort — supported by Kamalamai Municipality and community stakeholders — to widen access to science education in Sindhuli District.

The campus is affiliated with Tribhuvan University for bachelor-level study and with the National Examinations Board for +2 education, and it is recognised by the University Grants Commission of Nepal. Teaching is focused on the sciences at both levels, supported by laboratories, a library and computer facilities for regular coursework and practicals.

Alongside teaching, the campus extends scholarship support to students from marginalised and economically weak backgrounds, and maintains basic academic and student-support facilities for regular study.

Programs

Two routes through science

Students can join after Grade 10 for higher secondary science, and continue on the same campus into a four-year bachelor's degree.

NEB · 2 years

Ten Plus Two (+2) Science

Higher secondary science for Grade 11 and 12, following the National Examinations Board curriculum, with laboratory practicals in physics, chemistry and biology.

  • Level: Higher secondary (Grades 11–12)
  • Duration: 2 years, full-time
  • Board: National Examinations Board (NEB)
  • Entry: after SEE / Grade 10
Program details →
Tribhuvan University · 4 years

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) General

A four-year, full-time B.Sc. General program under Tribhuvan University's Institute of Science and Technology, taught in English, with 50 seats.

  • Level: Bachelor's degree
  • Duration: 4 years, full-time
  • Seats: 50
  • Entry: Grade 12 / higher secondary or equivalent
