Messages

From the Principal of Shree Kamala Secondary School and the Coordinator of the +2 Science programme.

Mr. Kamlesh Chaudhary

Principal, Shree Kamala Secondary School

A chemist by training, Mr. Chaudhary has spent more than 23 years teaching and guiding +2 Science students. He has played a leading part in making Shree Kamala Secondary School a science hub for students who go on to higher education in science.

Message from the Principal

Dear students, parents and well-wishers,

Welcome to +2 Science at Shree Kamala Secondary School! For more than seven decades this school has opened doors for the young people of Dhungrebas and Sindhuli — and today it is a place where students come to dream big in science.

I have spent more than twenty-three years in the chemistry classroom and laboratory with +2 students, and I can tell you what never stops amazing me: the moment a student realises they can understand the world, not just memorise it. That moment is why we are here. Whether you have chosen the Biology group or the Computer Science group, these two years will stretch you — and they will show you how much you are capable of.

So be curious. Ask “why?” in every class. Treat every practical as an experiment of your own. Work steadily, help the friend beside you, and never be afraid to come to your teachers — our doors are always open. Our students have gone on to higher education in science, and I have every confidence that you will too.

To parents: thank you for trusting us with your children’s future. Together, we will help them build it.

I wish every one of you a joyful, hard-working and successful journey through +2 Science. The future of Sindhuli’s science begins in these classrooms — and it begins with you.

Kamlesh Chaudhary Principal, Shree Kamala Secondary School

Mr. Bharat Malla

Coordinator, +2 Science

Mr. Malla holds a master’s degree in mathematics and has more than 20 years of experience teaching it — and he is among the most loved and appreciated teachers his students have had. He coordinates the +2 Science programme, and is also the Campus Chief of Kamala Science Campus.

Message from the Coordinator

Dear +2 Science students,

Welcome — I am truly glad you are here! Class 11 and Class 12 Science are two of the most exciting years of your education. This is where physics, chemistry, biology, computing and mathematics stop being subjects to pass and become tools you can think with.

In more than twenty years of teaching mathematics, I have learned one thing above all: every student can do it. I have watched students who were afraid of numbers become the ones solving problems on the board for everyone else. Confidence is not something you are born with — it is something you build, one solved problem at a time.

So here is my advice. Come to every class. Practise a little every single day instead of everything the night before an exam. Do your practicals with your own hands. And when you are stuck — and everyone gets stuck — ask. Your teachers are here for you, and so am I.

Your future is closer than you think. When you finish +2, a whole world of higher education in science is waiting — including a B.Sc. right here in Dhungrebas at Kamala Science Campus. Wherever you go, the foundation you build with us will carry you.

Work hard, stay curious, look after one another — and enjoy the journey. I can’t wait to see what you will achieve.

Bharat Malla Coordinator, +2 Science
