The teachers behind the programme

The people who teach and guide Class 11 and Class 12 Science at Shree Kamala Secondary School.

Leadership

Mr. Kamlesh Chaudhary

Principal · Chemistry

A chemist with more than 23 years of teaching and guiding +2 Science students, Mr. Chaudhary has played a leading part in making the school a science hub for students heading to higher education in science. Read his message.

Mr. Bharat Malla

+2 Coordinator · Mathematics

Mr. Malla holds a master’s degree in mathematics and has taught it for more than 20 years, and is among the teachers his students love and appreciate most. He is also the Campus Chief of Kamala Science Campus. Read his message.

Teaching faculty

Physics

Mr. Chandeshwar Yadav

Chemistry
