National Examinations Board · 2 years

Ten Plus Two (+2) Science

The +2 Science program covers Grades 11 and 12 under the National Examinations Board curriculum. It is the standard route into bachelor-level science, engineering, medical and allied programs in Nepal, and prepares students for the NEB board examinations at the end of each grade.

Teaching combines classroom instruction with laboratory practicals, using the campus science labs. Students who complete the program at Kamala Science Campus can continue directly into the B.Sc. General program here.

What students study

  • Compulsory subjects as prescribed by the NEB for the science stream, including English and Nepali.
  • Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics with laboratory work.
  • Biology or Computer Science as the optional subject, depending on the student's intended path.
  • Practical and internal assessment alongside the board examinations.

Subject combinations are offered according to the NEB structure and the campus timetable for each intake. Confirm the available combination with the campus office before applying.

Tribhuvan University · 4 years

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) General

The B.Sc. General program is a four-year, full-time undergraduate degree run under Tribhuvan University's Institute of Science and Technology (IOST). It is taught in English, and the campus offers 50 seats in the program.

The degree is built around a broad science foundation in the first years, narrowing towards a chosen major subject with project or field work in the final year. Alongside the core sciences, the curriculum includes scientific communication, applied statistics, research methodology and a computational course — the skills that carry into postgraduate study or scientific work.

Core subject areas

The B.Sc. General curriculum draws on the following subject areas. The combination available in a given intake depends on the subjects running at the campus that year.

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Mathematics
  • Botany
  • Zoology
  • Microbiology
  • Environmental Science
  • Geology
  • Meteorology
  • Statistics
  • Scientific Communication

How the four years are structured

  1. Year 1 — foundation

    Three core science subjects, plus Scientific Communication.

  2. Year 2 — consolidation

    The core subjects continue, with Applied Statistics added.

  3. Year 3 — specialisation begins

    Two core subjects carry forward, alongside Research Methodology and elective courses.

  4. Year 4 — major and project

    One major subject, a project or field work, a computational course and an interdisciplinary course.

After the degree

Where a B.Sc. General can lead

Postgraduate study

An M.Sc. in the major subject at Tribhuvan University or another university, and from there research or academic work.

Teaching

Science teaching at secondary and higher secondary level, often combined with a B.Ed. or teaching licence.

Applied and technical roles

Laboratory, environmental, agricultural, public-health and quality-control roles in government offices, NGOs and private organisations.
